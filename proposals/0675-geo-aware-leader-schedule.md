## Summary

A geo-aware leader schedule: the leader schedule is reordered so that
consecutive leaders tend to be geographically close, reducing the
average leader handoff latency. It consumes the validator locations and
the integer distance measure specified in [SIMD-0674] (Validator
Location Registration).

This is a placeholder to reserve the SIMD number; the full proposal
text follows shortly.
